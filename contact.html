Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting G-TEC Ottapalam.

Where exactly inside JRJ Complex is G-TEC situated?

We are located on the 2nd Floor of JRJ Complex, directly inside the Ottapalam Municipal Bus Stand Shopping Complex. Signboards are visible from the entrance courtyard, and staircase / elevator access is available.

Do I need prior appointment for career counselling?

No appointment is needed! Walk-ins are warmly welcomed anytime between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. However, submitting the online enquiry form beforehand ensures an expert in your specific domain is available to meet you.

What are the available batch timings?

We offer flexible batch schedules tailored for college students, job seekers, and working professionals: Morning (9:00 AM – 1:00 PM), Afternoon (1:30 PM – 5:30 PM), Evening (5:00 PM – 7:30 PM), as well as dedicated Weekend batches.

Are placement assistance services provided?

Yes, G-TEC Ottapalam provides 100% placement support focused across Palakkad district businesses, industrial enterprises, accounting firms, and regional technology offices. We conduct resume building, interview practice, and direct employer interviews.
