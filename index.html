About G-TEC Computer Education

Kerala’s Premier Institute for Job-Ready Skills Since 1999

Centrally located at the Bus Stand Complex in Ottapalam, G-TEC Computer Education has spent over 25 years transforming students, job seekers, and working professionals into confident industry specialists through rigorous, hands-on technical training.

100% Practical Lab Training

Every learner practices on dedicated workstations with authentic software licenses. No rote theory — you build genuine code, models, and ledgers from day one.

Globally Validated Credentials

ISO 9001:2015 certified programs, NSIC recognition, and SAP partner standards. Our certificates include instant online QR code verification recognized worldwide.

Dedicated Placement Cell

Full career support including portfolio reviews, technical interview mockups, soft-skills workshops, and direct hiring connections across Kerala and the GCC.
